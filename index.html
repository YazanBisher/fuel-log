    Nutrition data sources

    USDA FoodData Central covers whole and generic foods. The app uses the shared DEMO_KEY, which allows about 30 searches an hour. For more, get a free key at fdc.nal.usda.gov and paste it here.

    Open Food Facts handles barcode lookups first. It is a free, crowd-sourced database of over 3 million products and needs no key. If a barcode isn't there, the app checks USDA's branded foods.
